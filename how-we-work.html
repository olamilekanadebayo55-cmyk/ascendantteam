How we work

From enquiry to next step.

The process depends on the service you request. This is what to expect, in order.

01

Understand

Learn about the individual’s background, interests and goals.

02

Research

Explore relevant information and possible professional directions.

03

Guide

Help the individual interpret options and identify practical next steps.

04

Connect

Where a relevant and legitimate connection is available, explain the process and obtain permission before sharing personal information.

01

Submit an enquiry

Use the contact form to choose a topic and tell us what you are working toward. There is no charge for sending an enquiry.

02

Tell us what we need to know

We may ask about your background, goals, location and the specific question. For organizations, we ask about the scope of the research. We ask only for what we need.

03

We clarify your needs

We reply by email to confirm we have understood and to say whether the request is within our services.

04

Research, guidance or sourcing support

What happens next depends on the service you request. See each service for what it involves.

05

You receive our findings

We share findings or recommendations in writing and, where useful, in conversation. Sources and the dates we checked them are shown.

06

Proposed connections

Only where a legitimate connection exists. We explain who the other party is, what would be shared and why, and we wait for your explicit permission.

07

What happens next

The decision is yours. We outline your options, and there is no obligation to continue.

08

The limits of our services

We cannot guarantee interviews, offers, placements, compensation or selection by any employer. Results depend on your circumstances and on decisions that others make.

Where a service is paid, we disclose pricing and deliverables before you commit.

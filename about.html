About Us

Research, guidance and responsible connections.

Ascendant Talent helps professionals navigate their career direction, make informed decisions and explore relevant opportunities through research, guidance and responsible professional connections.

What Ascendant Talent is

A talent research and career guidance service. We combine relevant information, honest perspective and clear next steps, and we treat each person’s goals, experience and circumstances as the starting point.

Ascendant Groups and Ascendant Career Services

Ascendant Talent operates under Ascendant Groups. Résumé writing and career-document services are provided by a separate brand, Ascendant Career Services, and are not part of Ascendant Talent.

Visit Ascendant Career Services

Why it exists

Career questions rarely have tidy answers, and the information people rely on is often scattered, out of date or written to sell something. We built Ascendant Talent on a simple belief: people decide better when they have relevant information, honest perspective and control over how their details are used.

What we do and how

Our services cover career guidance, career research, professional development and, where offered, talent research for organizations. Our process runs from understanding your situation to research, guidance and, only where legitimate and with your consent, a professional connection.

What you can expect

  • A reply to your enquiry by email.
  • A plain statement of what is and is not within our services.
  • Any price and deliverables stated before you commit.
  • Your consent before we share your personal information with anyone.

What you should not expect

  • Guaranteed interviews, offers, placements or compensation.
  • Representation of an employer, unless authorization is verified and stated.
  • Résumé writing, which is provided by Ascendant Career Services.
  • Legal, financial or medical advice.

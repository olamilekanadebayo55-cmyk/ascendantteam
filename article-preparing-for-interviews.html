Preparing for an interview with substance

Ascendant Talent · October 2026

Read the vacancy line by line and note the three or four capabilities it stresses most. For each, prepare one specific example: the situation, what you did, and the result.

Research the employer through its own publications, recent announcements and the people you will meet. Prepare questions that show you have done so, such as how the team measures success in the first year.

Practice answering aloud, keep answers to about two minutes, and be ready to discuss gaps or changes in your history plainly. Confirm the format, time zone and attendees in advance.

General guidance only. It is not advice tailored to your situation and does not guarantee any outcome.
